# 💼 Accountant

## Overview

**Role ID**: `accountant`  
**Category**: Finance  
**Access Level**: 3 (High - Financial reporting)  
**License Requirement**: Base System (Included)

---

## Description

Accountant chịu trách nhiệm financial reporting, month-end close, reconciliation, và compliance. Làm việc với [[Finance Officer]] để đảm bảo financial accuracy và preparing reports cho management.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Financial Reporting (🤖 AI-Enhanced)
- Monthly financial close và reconciliation
- Generate financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
- Tax preparation và compliance
- Audit coordination
- Financial analysis và reporting
- Budget tracking và variance analysis
- **🆕 AI-powered automated report generation**
- **🆕 Predictive financial modeling**
- **🆕 Automated variance analysis**
- **🆕 AI-detected anomalies and irregularities**

### 2. Compliance & Auditing (🤖 AI-Enhanced)
- **🆕 AI-powered compliance checking**
- **🆕 Automated audit trail tracking**
- **🆕 Smart tax calculation and reporting**
- **🆕 Risk detection and alerts**

### 3. Financial Analysis (🤖 AI-Enhanced)
- **🆕 AI-powered trend analysis**
- **🆕 Revenue forecasting and projections**
- **🆕 Cost optimization recommendations**
- **🆕 Profitability analysis by program/branch**

### 4. AI Accounting Assistant (New!)
- **🤖 Automated Reconciliation**: Match transactions automatically
- **🤖 Predictive Analytics**: Forecast revenue, expenses
- **🤖 Anomaly Detection**: Flag unusual transactions
- **🤖 Smart Reports**: Auto-generate financial reports
- **🤖 Budget Insights**: Track and predict budget variance
