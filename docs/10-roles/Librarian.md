# 📖 Librarian

## Overview

**Role ID**: `librarian`  
**Category**: Academic  
**Access Level**: 2 (Moderate - Content management)  
**License Requirement**: Base System (Included)

---

## Description

Librarian quản lý learning content library, curate resources, organize materials, và ensure content quality. Supports teachers và students trong accessing educational resources.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Digital Library Management (🤖 AI-Enhanced)
- Upload và manage learning content (video, document, audio, interactive, ebook)
- **🆕 Monitor AI-powered content processing**
- Curate và organize digital resources
- Maintain content library structure
- **🆕 Review AI-generated tags and categories**
- **🆕 Approve AI content recommendations**
- Quality check materials for accuracy
- **🆕 Monitor content usage analytics**

### 2. Content Organization (🤖 AI-Enhanced)
- Categorize resources by topic, level, industry
- **🆕 Verify AI auto-categorization**
- Tag content with relevant keywords
- **🆕 Review and refine AI-generated tags**
- Create learning pathways and playlists
- **🆕 Use AI recommendations for related content**

### 3. Content Processing (New!)
- **🆕 Upload content to Digital Library**
- **🆕 Monitor video transcoding progress**
- **🆕 Review AI-generated transcripts**
- **🆕 Approve AI-generated summaries**
- **🆕 Set content access levels (public/enrolled/class/paid)**

### 4. User Support (🤖 AI-Enhanced)
- Assist teachers và students in finding resources
- Handle content requests
- **🆕 Use AI semantic search to find relevant content**
- **🆕 Provide personalized recommendations**
- Manage content permissions and access

### 5. Quality Assurance (🤖 AI-Enhanced)
- Review uploaded content quality
- Remove outdated or duplicate resources
- **🆕 Monitor AI content quality scores**
- **🆕 Review user ratings and feedback**
- **🆕 Analyze content effectiveness metrics**

### 6. AI Library Features (New!)
- **🤖 Review AI Tags**: Verify auto-generated tags
- **🤖 Content Analytics**: Track views, completion rates
- **🤖 Recommendation Engine**: Oversee AI suggestions
- **🤖 Search Optimization**: Improve semantic search results
- **🤖 Difficulty Assessment**: Review AI difficulty scores
