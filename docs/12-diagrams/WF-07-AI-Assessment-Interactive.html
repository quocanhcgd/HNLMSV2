📊 AI Essay Feedback Example

Bài luận: "My Hometown"

📊 Overall Score: 32/40 (80%)
Criterion Score Comments
Content 8/10 Good ideas, relevant examples
Grammar 7/10 Minor tense errors
Structure 9/10 Clear paragraphs, good flow
Vocabulary 8/10 Good variety, some repetition

✓ Strengths:

  • Clear thesis statement in introduction
  • Good use of descriptive adjectives
  • Well-organized paragraphs

⚠️ Areas to Improve:

  • Tense consistency: "I live" vs "I lived" - be consistent with present tense
  • Word repetition: "beautiful" used 5 times - try: gorgeous, stunning, picturesque
  • Conclusion: Restate main points more clearly

💡 Suggestions:

  • Add more specific details about your hometown
  • Use transition words: Furthermore, Moreover, In addition
  • Practice past tense for narrative sections
